# Limitations
Because of the method used to predetermine the facing and moving direction, only the World 1 hammer brother can be predetermined, you can only predetermine the facing and moving direction after completing levels and not after dying in levels, and the facing and moving direction cannot be predetermined after completing 1-F. You can predetermine hammer brothers of other worlds, but only the one in the first slot, and you can't predetermine hammer brothers after completing fortresses or other hammer brothers.
